# The Shattered Pendulum: World of Vasrenthia

## Creation Myth

The people of Vasrenthia do not speak of gods creating the world. They speak instead of the **Pendulum** — a vast, unknowable mechanism that once swung between two states of existence: **Stillness** and **Motion**. In the age before memory, the Pendulum cracked. Time itself splintered. Some places in Vasrenthia now move too quickly — forests that grow, die, and regrow in a single afternoon. Others have nearly frozen — valleys where a raindrop hangs suspended for decades. The world exists in the fractured aftermath, and its people have learned to navigate the temporal wounds like sailors charting reefs.

No one knows what broke the Pendulum. The oldest texts, etched on slowstone tablets in the Hanging Archives, suggest it was not an act of violence but of *grief* — that some foundational sorrow resonated at the exact frequency of the mechanism and shattered it. Scholars call this the **First Lament**, and many believe that if its cause could be understood, time itself might be healed.

## The Magic System: Cadence

Magic in Vasrenthia is called **Cadence** — the art of manipulating temporal flow. Practitioners, known as **Metronomists**, do not conjure fire or hurl lightning. Instead, they accelerate rot in an enemy's armor, slow the velocity of an incoming arrow, age a seed into a tree in moments, or preserve a dying body in a bubble of near-stillness until a healer arrives. Cadence is performed through rhythmic patterns: drumming, humming, tapping, even heartbeat regulation. The more precise the rhythm, the more powerful the effect.

However, Cadence exacts a cost called **Drift**. Each use slightly desynchronizes the practitioner from the normal flow of time. Minor Drift manifests as flickering — the Metronomist appears to stutter, existing in two moments at once. Severe Drift can exile someone into a temporal eddy permanently, visible but unreachable, frozen mid-step for eternity. The roads of Vasrenthia are dotted with such figures, called the **Still Ones**, serving as grim warnings.

## Geography

### 1. The Rust Marches
A vast expanse of red-orange grassland where time moves slightly faster than normal. Metal corrodes rapidly here, forcing inhabitants to build with bone, glass, and lacquered wood. The nomadic **Corroden clans** traverse these plains on great arthropod mounts called skethren. This is where the player begins.

### 2. The Hanging Archives of Peltravar
A city built vertically into and upon a colossal cliff face. Home to the world's greatest repository of knowledge, maintained by the **Order of the Unblinking**, scholars who use minor Cadence to slow their own aging in exchange for lifetimes of study. The city is layered — the wealthier and more knowledgeable live higher.

### 3. The Verdance
A jungle where time spirals erratically. Trees grow to enormous height in hours, then collapse into mulch, feeding the next generation. Creatures here have evolved to live fast — hatching, growing, reproducing, and dying in days. The indigenous **Bloomborn** people navigate using temporal maps that chart the jungle's shifting rhythms.

### 4. The Glassfield of Ometh
A silent desert of fused sand where time has nearly stopped. Walking through it feels like wading through honey. At its center lies the **Fulcrum Shard**, believed to be a physical fragment of the broken Pendulum. Multiple factions seek to control it.

### 5. The Tidelock Isles
An archipelago where each island exists in a different season simultaneously. Bridges of frozen seawater connect winter isles to summer ones. The isles are governed by the **Commodore Parliament**, a democracy of merchant-captains whose economy depends on exploiting temporal differentials for trade — aging wine in hours, preserving perishable goods indefinitely.

## Major Historical Events

- **The First Lament** (Year Zero): The Pendulum breaks. Time fractures across the world.
- **The Metronomic Accord** (Year 312): Cadence practitioners codify ethical rules limiting temporal manipulation on living beings.
- **The War of Seconds** (Year 587): The Tidelock Isles and Peltravar fight a devastating conflict over access to the Glassfield. Ends in stalemate.
- **The Stillfall** (Year 891): A catastrophic event in which an entire Corroden clan is frozen mid-migration across the Rust Marches. Thousands of Still Ones, locked in time, remain standing in formation. No explanation has ever been found.
- **Present Day** (Year 923): Tremors of temporal instability are increasing worldwide. Some believe a **Second Lament** is coming.

## Current Political Tensions

The Order of the Unblinking has detected accelerating temporal fractures and issued a dire warning: the Fulcrum Shard is destabilizing. The Commodore Parliament denies this, fearing economic collapse if the Glassfield is restricted. The Corroden clans, still haunted by the Stillfall, have begun mobilizing — though whether for war or exodus, no one is certain. Meanwhile, a secretive group called the **Lament Seekers** believes the Second Lament is not a disaster but a *correction*, and they are actively trying to trigger it. In the margins of these grand conflicts, people vanish. Time pools like standing water in forgotten places. And somewhere in the deep silence of Ometh, the Fulcrum Shard hums a note that no one can quite identify.
